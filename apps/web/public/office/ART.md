# Office art: where it came from

Everything here is drawn here, by a script in this repo, or generated by us in Google Flow (the characters, floors, furniture and trees; see `art-source/flow/README.md`). There are no third-party assets: nothing is copied or downloaded from another product or another artist. Because it is ours there is no licence to track.

| File | What it is | Made by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tiles.png` | The eight floor, wall, carpet and door tiles the map uses, from a Google Flow swatch sheet (`art-source/flow/tiles.png`); the script's own flat tiles are the fallback. | `scripts/make-office.mjs`, then `scripts/import-flow-tiles.mjs` |
| `agents.png` | The agents, drawn as small hovering robots: five looks, three frames each, four directions, 32 by 32 frames. From Google Flow robot sheets (`art-source/flow/robot-0.png`, `robot-1.png`); the other looks are those robots with their colours turned. | `scripts/import-flow-robots.mjs` |
| `people.png` | You and the agents: six looks, three frames each (stand, step, step), four directions. 32 by 32 frames. Each look is a character generated in Google Flow (sheets in `art-source/flow/`), sliced in by the importer; a look with no sheet is drawn by the script. | `scripts/make-sprites.mjs`, then `scripts/import-flow-looks.mjs` |
| `objects.png` | One piece of furniture for each kind of object in the office (boards, desks, a monitor, a mailbox, a radio, tables), in the order of `OBJECT_TYPES`. 32 by 32 pieces in one row. From two Google Flow sheets (`objects-a.png`, `objects-b.png`). | `scripts/make-sprites.mjs`, then `scripts/import-flow-objects.mjs` |
| `decor/` | One picture for each piece of scenery (`desk.png`, `chair.png`, `sofa.png`, `tree.png` and so on) and for each thing you use (`obj-<type>.png`), top-down, each at its own size in tiles. From Google Flow sheets (`art-source/flow/decor-a.png`, `decor-b.png`, `objects-a.png`, `objects-b.png`). | `scripts/import-flow-decor.mjs`, `scripts/import-flow-objects.mjs` |
| `furniture.png` | A desk, a chair, a plant, a sofa, a table and a tree. 32 by 32 pieces in one row. From a Google Flow sheet (`art-source/flow/furniture.png`). | `scripts/make-sprites.mjs`, then `scripts/import-flow-objects.mjs` |

To use other art (for example Kenney, CC0): replace a PNG with a sheet of the same layout and add a row here naming the file, its source and its licence. A test fails if a file is in this folder and not listed here.
