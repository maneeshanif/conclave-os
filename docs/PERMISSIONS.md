# Permissions to use other people's art

CLAUDE.md and WORLD-BRIEF forbid copying WorkAdventure code, tiles, sprites, maps, icons, fonts or text (AGPL v3 with the Commons Clause). Anees reports (2026-11-17) that WorkAdventure gave written permission by email to use its tree, floor and table art and "everything". That changes the rule only once the permission is on record here. Until the section below is filled in, no WorkAdventure file is copied into this repo.

## What to record (Anees, please paste it; keep it out of any public repo if the email has private details)

1. **Who gave it and when:** name or team at WorkAdventure, the date, and the address it came from.
2. **The exact words** of the permission (paste the email text under "Permission text" below).
3. **What it covers:** which files (for example the tree, floor and table tiles from `maps/assets`), whether it covers code as well as art, and whether it allows use in a product that is sold or run for others.
4. **Conditions:** credit wording, no resale of the tiles on their own, anything else they asked for.

## Why the exact words matter

- The permission has to come from whoever holds the rights. Many tilesets in a map pack are made by other artists under their own licences (some need a credit, some forbid resale), so WorkAdventure can only grant what it owns. The folders I looked at (`maps/assets`, `maps/starter`) have no per-file licence list, so the email is the only record of what is allowed.
- A reply that says "all things are fine" in general is not enough to ship a product; the line that names the art files is.
